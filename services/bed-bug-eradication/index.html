What Are Bed Bugs and How Do Infestations Spread Indoors?

Bed bugs are small, flat, reddish-brown insects, about the size of an apple seed. They feed on human blood, usually at night. They are not caused by dirt: they can travel on luggage, clothes, second-hand furniture, mattresses and bags, so clean homes and hotels can also get them.

Early Warning Signs of a Bed Bug Infestation on Mattresses &Seating

  • Itchy red bites in lines or clusters on arms, neck, back or legs
  • Small rust-coloured or dark spots on sheets, mattresses and pillows
  • Tiny white eggs or pale shed skins in seams, folds and corners
  • A sweet, musty smell in heavier infestations
  • Live bugs in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, sofas or behind frames
  • Disturbed sleep and bites that keep appearing

Bites can resemble other insect bites, so an inspection is the surest way to confirm bed bug activity.

Hidden Bed Bug Harborages in Bed Frames, Headboards, Sofas & Skirting

  • Mattress seams, tags and box springs
  • Bed frames, headboards and wooden joints
  • Sofas, recliners and cushions
  • Cracks in walls, skirting and behind picture frames
  • Curtains, carpets and bedside tables
  • Electrical sockets and switchboards near the bed
  • Luggage, bags and piles of clothes

Why DIY Sprays and Home Remedies Fail Against Bed Bug Eggs

Market sprays, sunlight, neem or washing sheets may kill some visible bugs, but often do not reach eggs and insects hidden deep inside furniture and walls. Because bed bugs can multiply quickly, surviving bugs may rebuild an infestation within weeks. Professional treatment targets hiding places and uses a planned follow-up to check for bugs that hatch after the first visit.

Practical Steps to Prevent Bed Bugs Returning via Luggage & Furniture

  • Inspect mattress seams and bed frames regularly.
  • Check hotel beds and headboards when travelling, and keep luggage off the bed.
  • Wash and heat-dry clothes and bedding after trips.
  • Inspect second-hand furniture before bringing it home.
  • Reduce clutter near the bed to limit hiding places.
  • Seal cracks in walls and around skirting.
  • Use mattress and pillow covers that zip fully.

What to Expect During a Professional Bed Bug Room Inspection

Matching Treatment Methods to Mattresses, Upholstery & Wooden Joints

Bed-bug control may involve different approaches, but a method should be chosen only after the room, furnishings and access conditions are assessed. Heat treatment can be discussed where the service is available and the affected items can safely tolerate the process. Ask what parts of the room are included, how heat-sensitive belongings will be handled and which preparation instructions apply. Do not use household heaters or improvise a high-temperature treatment; an uncontrolled approach can create safety and property risks without confirming that hidden insects have been addressed.

Steam treatment may be considered for appropriate seams or surfaces when the material, moisture sensitivity and technician access permit it. The provider should explain the surfaces covered and any drying or room-use guidance. Steam should not be described as a whole-room treatment unless that is the actual method assessed and agreed. Residents should not independently steam electrical items, delicate materials or areas for which they have not received instructions.

A residual spray may be discussed for suitable locations if the product label and treatment plan allow it. Confirm which surfaces will be treated, how personal items and food are protected, and what access restrictions or re-entry instructions apply. A product should never be applied to bedding or other surfaces contrary to its label. The site team should communicate only the precautions provided for the chosen product and room.

These approaches are not interchangeable, and the availability of a particular method should be confirmed rather than assumed from a general service description. The inspection and written scope should identify the selected treatment and any follow-up requested under the service terms. How do I know if I have bed bugs?

Look for itchy bites, blood spots on sheets, tiny eggs and shed skins in mattress seams, and live bugs in bed frames and furniture. An inspection confirms activity.

Usually not. Professional treatment targets mattress seams and the bed frame. We will advise you if an item is too badly infested to save.

Yes. Bed bugs are not caused by dirt. They travel on luggage, clothes and furniture, so clean homes and hotels can also get them.
