Frequently Asked Questions About Scorpion Control in Rajasthan

What should I do if I see a scorpion indoors?

Keep people and pets away, do not handle it, and contact the service team to discuss an inspection. Seek urgent medical advice if anyone is stung.

Can a photograph confirm which scorpion is present?

A photograph from a safe distance may help describe the report, but an on-site assessment is needed to review the property and activity.

Will the whole property need treatment?

Not necessarily. The proposed scope should be based on the reported locations and inspection findings.

How should I prepare for an inspection?

Share where and when the scorpion was seen and any access limits. Do not move nearby items until the service team advises you.

Can businesses arrange scorpion service?

Yes. Describe occupancy, site operations and the affected spaces so access and precautions can be discussed.

Does an inspection include recurring visits?

Recurring visits are not assumed. Ask what may be included in a written Commercial AMC (IPM) scope.
