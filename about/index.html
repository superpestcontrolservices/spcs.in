Our Story Protecting Rajasthan Since 1998 Founded in 1998 by Mr. Kishore Singh Chouhan, Super Pest Control Services began with a clear mission: to provide safe, reliable, and highly effective pest management to our community. Over the last two and a half decades, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in commercial and residential pest control across Rajasthan. We combine a deep-rooted legacy of customer care with modern, science-backed treatment methods. From precision termite treatments to advanced chemical formulations for lasting residual protection, we are committed to the same values Mr. Chouhan established over 25 years ago: integrity, technical expertise, and total peace of mind for our clients.

Super Pest Control is focused on providing professional pest control services for residential and commercial customers.

We believe in reliable service, professional solutions and a customer-first approach.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver targeted, highly effective pest eradication and prevention protocols for both residential homes and complex commercial facilities. We are committed to solving immediate infestations and providing long-term peace of mind by:

01

Implementing Advanced Protocols

Utilizing meticulously calibrated, industry-grade formulations, including strategic AlphaMethrin, Propoxur, and Deltamethrin applications, to completely eliminate source infestations of cockroaches, termites, bed bugs, and rodents.

02

Prioritizing Absolute Safety

Safeguarding the health of families, pets, and employees by strictly adhering to safe dilution ratios and applying treatments strategically away from food preparation and high-traffic areas.

03

Leveraging Local Expertise

Applying our hyper-local understanding of the specific seasonal pest pressures across Jaipur and Beawar to deliver rapid, reliable, and discrete extermination services.

04

Fortifying Properties

Moving beyond simple extermination by actively identifying hidden nesting sites and structural entry points to protect everything from private villas and flats to large-scale factories and corporate workspaces against future invasions.
