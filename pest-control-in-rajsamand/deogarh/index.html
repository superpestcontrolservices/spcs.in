Safety Precautions & Post-Treatment Care for Deogarh Properties

Mosquito source reduction

Mosquito management includes checking for standing water where larvae may develop and keeping water containers covered or emptied where practical. Door and window screens can also help limit entry indoors.

Fogging may be one part of a broader vector-control approach, but it does not remove breeding sources. Ask the service team which measures suit the site and current concern.

Wood borer signs

Small exit holes or fine powder around timber can have several causes and do not by themselves identify a wood-boring pest. Note the affected item and location, and avoid applying an unverified product to the wood.

An inspection can help identify the cause and determine whether wood borer control is suitable. Treatment recommendations depend on the material and extent of the issue.
