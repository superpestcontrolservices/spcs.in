Questions for venue operators

Does outdoor fogging remove mosquito breeding sources?

No. Fogging does not eliminate water-holding sources; ask the technician whether source assessment or another method is suitable.

Can the pest provider certify a venue’s HACCP system?

No. The provider may discuss pest-control documentation, but formal certification is issued by an accredited certification body.
