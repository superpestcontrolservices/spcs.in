Questions food teams often raise

Should a pest sighting be treated as proof that a food batch is unsafe?

A sighting alone does not establish product impact. Follow the business’s own food-safety procedure, record what was observed and ask the responsible food-safety lead to assess the affected area or goods.

Can a pest-control provider issue our FSSAI licence or HACCP certificate?

No. The provider can help with pest-related documentation, implementation discussions and audit preparation. Licensing decisions belong to the authority, and certification is issued by an accredited certification body.
